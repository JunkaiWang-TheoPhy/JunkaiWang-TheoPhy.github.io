### [1] [Scattering by a medium with self-similar or fractal structure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12728)
*Habib Ammari,Yat Tin Chow,Fuqun Han*

Main category: math.SP

TL;DR: 本文构建了一个用于自相似或预分形（prefractal）几何介质中波散射的拓扑学框架，通过在对数尺度上周期化的辅助边界积分模型结合Bloch-Floquet-Zak变换，证明了精确Riesz约化族与投影矩阵的行列式绕转一致，并给出了正多单形构型下的非零绕转公式及Zak相位的量化关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自相似或预分形几何介质的波散射问题缺乏统一的拓扑分析框架，物理尺度恒等关系暗示存在深层结构，但理论上尚未被严格阐明。

Method: 利用物理尺度恒等关系构造在对数尺度上周期化的辅助边界积分模型；通过Bloch-Floquet-Zak变换实现跨尺度耦合的纤维化；对充分小且良好分离的组分定义平衡密度以构建有限维投影矩阵，并以Riesz投影选取全边界符号的对应不变谱子空间。

Result: 在谱隔离与点间隙条件下，证明了精确Riesz约化族的行列式绕转与投影矩阵一致；针对正多单形构型得到了非零绕转公式并追踪了有限预分形层级及几何波数列上的局域绕转数据；给出了不同膨胀中心的条件性结果；证明手性厄米化的Zak相位等于点间隙绕转乘以π模2π。

Conclusion: 尺度周期边界模型具备严格的拓扑约化性质，为自相似介质波散射提供了一套完整的拓扑不变量描述框架。

Abstract: We develop a topological framework for wave scattering by a medium with self-similar or prefractal geometry. Physical scaling identities motivate an auxiliary boundary-integral model that is periodic in logarithmic scale, and the Bloch-Floquet-Zak transform fiberizes its interscale coupling. For sufficiently small, well-separated components, equilibrium densities define a computable finite-dimensional projected matrix, while Riesz projections select the corresponding invariant spectral subspace of the full boundary symbol. Under the stated spectral-isolation and point-gap conditions, we prove that the determinant winding of the exact Riesz-reduced family agrees with that of the projected matrix. For the regular-simplex configurations, we obtain nonzero winding formulas and track the resulting local winding data across finite prefractal levels and along a geometric sequence of wavenumbers. We also obtain conditional results for different dilation centers and show that the Zak phase of the chiral Hermitianization is equal to $π$ times the point-gap winding modulo $2π$. Thus, the scale-periodic boundary model admits a rigorous topological reduction.

### [2] [Blinking membrane patterns induced by protein binding/unbinding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13016)
*Hiroshi Noguchi*

Main category: cond-mat.soft

TL;DR: 本文利用无网格膜模拟（meshless membrane simulation）研究非平衡态膜图案形成，发现蛋白质的循环结合/解离可通过离格活性Potts模型（off-lattice active Potts model）机制诱导闪烁域（blinking domains），且此类结构即使在无张力膜上也能形成。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探讨非平衡条件下膜图案形成的机制，特别是周期性蛋白结合/解离如何影响膜上结构域的行为，并比较热平衡与非平衡条件下结构域形成的条件差异。

Method: 采用无网格膜模拟方法，将膜上结合蛋白建模为具有两种不同自发曲率（spontaneous curvature）的状态，利用化学势差驱动的蛋白结合/解离循环，构建离格活性Potts模型进行模拟研究。

Result: 发现蛋白质循环结合/解离可诱导闪烁域，其尺寸随时间振荡：高自发曲率蛋白形成凸域后，蛋白转变为低自发曲率状态导致域收缩，此过程反复进行。在热平衡下，六边形凸域由弯曲能与表面张力能的竞争形成，仅在正表面张力下稳定存在；但闪烁域即使在无张力膜上也能形成。

Conclusion: 非平衡的周期性蛋白结合/解离为膜图案形成提供了与热平衡截然不同的机制，使得结构域能够在缺乏表面张力支撑的条件下依然形成，拓展了对活细胞膜动态行为的理解。

Abstract: Nonequilibrium membrane pattern formation is studied using meshless membrane simulation. Bound proteins are considered to have two states that generate different membrane spontaneous curvatures. Protein binding and unbinding occur cyclically owing to chemical potential differences, as an off-lattice active Potts model. It is found that this cyclic binding/unbinding can induce blinking domains, with oscillating size: convex domains of the proteins with a higher spontaneous curvature grow, and subsequently, the proteins change to the other state with a lower spontaneous curvature, resulting in domain shrinkage. These processes repeat. In thermal equilibrium, hexagonal convex domains are formed by the competition between bending and surface tension energies, so that they are stably formed only under positive surface tension. However, blinking domains can form even in tensionless membranes.

### [3] [Substrate-Directed Wetting Layers in Bicontinuous Particle-Stabilised Emulsions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13230)
*Jesse M. Steenhoff,Martin F. Haase*

Main category: cond-mat.soft

TL;DR: 本研究通过溶剂转移诱导相分离（solvent-transfer-induced phase separation, STrIPS）在具有可控润湿性的硅烷功能化玻璃基底上制备双连续界面堵塞乳液凝胶（bijel）薄膜，并结合共聚焦显微镜和数值模拟揭示了基底性质对成膜动力学和最终形貌的调控作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: STrIPS技术虽能实现双连续界面堵塞乳液凝胶（bicontinuous interfacially jammed emulsion gels, bijels）薄膜在固体基底上的连续大规模制备，但基底性质对成膜动力学和最终形貌的影响机制尚不明确，制约了该类功能材料的应用开发。

Method: 在硅烷功能化玻璃基底上制备具有选择性可控润湿性的STrIPS bijel薄膜，利用共聚焦显微镜（confocal microscopy）分析所得结构，并将实验结果与数值模拟（numerical simulations）进行对照验证。

Result: 结果显示bijel-基底界面处存在显著的润湿层（wetting layers），其厚度可通过纳米颗粒质量分数进行调控；与数值模拟一致，基底疏水性的增强驱动了从层状、富水表面结构向斑块状、逐渐富油结构的转变。

Conclusion: 本研究发现为基底在支撑型bijel薄膜中的结构导向作用提供了关键见解，有助于推动其作为功能材料的实际应用。

Abstract: Bicontinuous interfacially jammed emulsion gels (bijels) facilitate efficient mass transport across multiple length scales due to their interwoven structure of particle-stabilised liquid channels. This unique morphology imparts considerable potential for applications in separation and catalysis, particularly when fabricated \textit{via} solvent-transfer-induced phase separation (STrIPS). STrIPS enables the continuous, large-scale production of nanostructured bijel films on solid substrates, yet the influence of the substrate properties on the formation dynamics and final morphology remains insufficiently understood. In this study, this relationship is elucidated by preparing STrIPS bijel films on silane-functionalised glass substrates with selectively controlled wettability and analysing the resulting structure with confocal microscopy. The results showed the presence of notable wetting layers at the bijel-substrate interface, whose thicknesses could be tuned through the nanoparticle weight fraction. In line with numerical simulations, increasing the substrate hydrophobicity drove a transition from a laminar, water-rich surface layer to a patch-like, progressively oil-rich structure. These findings provide crucial insight into the structure-directing role of substrates in supported bijel films, which aids their application as functional materials.

### [4] [Thermodynamic formalism for intermittent maps with multiple neutral fixed points and phase transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12784)
*Daniel Coronel,Francisco Monardes*

Main category: math.DS

TL;DR: 本文为具有有限个中性不动点（neutral fixed points）的间歇性全分支区间映射（intermittent full-branch interval maps）及一类可能不连续的势函数（potentials）建立了热力学形式主义（thermodynamic formalism）。在势函数的双曲性假设下，证明了唯一平衡态（equilibrium state）的存在性，具有正熵及指数相关衰减，并刻画了单参数族 βφ 的相变（phase transitions）行为。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 针对具有间歇性行为的动力系统，其中性不动点使得经典热力学形式主义失效，需要专门的数学框架来处理。本研究旨在推广热力学形式主义至更广泛的、允许不连续势函数的间歇全分支区间映射，并深入了解此类系统中平衡态的唯一性与相变结构。

Method: 方法基于共形测度（conformal measures）的详细分析。通过对势函数施加双曲性假设，结合共形测度工具来建立平衡态的存在性、熵性质及相关衰减，并借助单参数族 βφ 考察相变临界行为。

Result: 在势函数满足双曲性假设时，证明了唯一平衡态的存在性，且该平衡态具有正熵和指数相关衰减。对于单参数族 βφ（β>0），刻画了相变行为：在临界参数下，可能同时存在多个遍历平衡态（ergodic equilibrium states），其中至多一个具有正熵。

Conclusion: 本文建立了间歇全分支区间映射在较广势类下的热力学形式主义基础，揭示出在临界参数处多个平衡态并存的相变现象，且正熵平衡态至多唯一，深化了对此类动力系统平衡态结构的理解。

Abstract: In this paper, we develop the thermodynamic formalism for intermittent full-branch interval maps with finitely many neutral fixed points and for a broad class of possibly discontinuous potentials. Under a hyperbolicity assumption on the potential, we prove the existence of a unique equilibrium state with positive entropy and exponential decay of correlations. Furthermore, we characterize the phase transitions for the one-parameter family $β\varphi$, with $β>0$, and prove that, at the critical parameter, multiple ergodic equilibrium states may coexist, at most one of which has positive entropy. Our approach relies on a detailed analysis of conformal measures.

</details>
