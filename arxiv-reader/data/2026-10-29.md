### [1] [Same-spin correlation screens the exchange peak of the electron gas near 2kF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35896)
*Carl A. Kukkonen*

Main category: cond-mat.supr-con

TL;DR: 本文用最低阶图（含动态屏蔽相互作用、无拟合参数）同时计算电子气两个自旋通道的局域场因子（local field factor），说明计入平行自旋关联后峰值被移除约一半，并与量子蒙特卡罗（quantum Monte Carlo, QMC）数据吻合。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 仅考虑交换作用（exchange）会给出在略低于 2kF 处出现尖峰的局域场因子，而量子蒙特卡罗结果未显示该尖峰，二者存在明显矛盾，需要澄清该差异的来源。

Method: 基于动态屏蔽相互作用（dynamically screened interaction）的最低阶图，分别计算两个自旋通道的贡献，不引入拟合参数；所用两类图对应 Hedin 顶点方程（Hedin's vertex equation）核的两项，其中第二项通常被忽略而本文予以保留。

Result: 平行自旋关联（parallel spin correlation）是交换作用的负像，可移除约一半的峰值；所计算的自旋分辨局域场因子在 rs = 1 与 2 处可解释 QMC 数据，并在 q = 0 处复现由基态能量给出的压缩率（compressibility）与自旋磁化率（spin susceptibility）。

Conclusion: 交换作用导致的局域场因子尖峰主要源于遗漏 Hedin 顶点方程核的第二项；将两类最低阶图一并纳入即可消除该表观矛盾并与 QMC 及热力学量一致。

Abstract: Exchange alone gives the electron gas a local field factor with a sharp peak just below 2kF. Quantum Monte Carlo finds none. Both spin channels are computed here from their lowest diagrams with a dynamically screened interaction and no fitting. Parallel spin correlation is the negative image of exchange and removes about half the peak; the calculated spin-resolved factors account for the QMC data at rs = 1 and 2, and at q = 0 for the compressibility and spin susceptibility obtained from the ground-state energy. The two classes of diagrams are the two terms of the kernel of Hedin's vertex equation, of which the second is routinely dropped.

### [2] [Cosmology of the Baby Universe in Coupled SYK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36032)
*Andrew Sontag*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: 本文分析耦合SYK实现AS²几何中“婴儿宇宙（baby universe）”的结构，借助关于多个Majorana算符迹的组合论证，得到有限N、p下有效且给出有限λ的弦规则完备，发现热AdS相与AS²相之间的一阶相变，并确认体宇宙学的大爆炸/大挤压性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有弦规则（chord rules）仅在特定极限下成立，缺少有限N、p与有限λ下的完整描述，因此需要发展对大量Majorana算符迹（traces of many Majoranas）的组合论证，以刻画耦合SYK框架中AS²几何所对应的婴儿宇宙结构。

Method: 采用组合论证（combinatorial arguments）分析多Majorana算符的迹，该类论证在有限N和p下仍然有效，从而给出先前弦规则的有限λ完备（finite λ completion）；以此研究热AdS相与AS²相之间的相变，并考察婴儿宇宙中的洛伦兹时间演化（Lorentzian time evolution）。

Result: 发现热AdS相与AS²相之间存在一阶相变（first order phase transition），该相变在β→∞极限下依然存在，并由重粒子插入（heavy particle insertion）的质量以及SYK与Maldacena-Qi耦合常数之比共同参数化；婴儿宇宙的洛伦兹时间演化给出余弦形式的标度因子（cosine scale factor）。

Conclusion: 组合方法可给出有限λ下弦规则的完备形式，并支持耦合SYK中婴儿宇宙具有一阶相变结构；余弦标度因子确认了该体宇宙学（bulk cosmology）的大爆炸/大挤压（big bang / big crunch）性质。

Abstract: We analyze the structure of the baby universe which arises in the coupled SYK realization of the AS$^2$ geometry. To do this, we develop and leverage combinatorial arguments about traces of many Majoranas which remain valid at finite $N$ and $p$ and thus furnish the finite $λ$ completion of the chord rules found in [1]. We find that there is a first order phase transition which persists in the $β\rightarrow\infty$ limit between the thermal AdS and AS$^2$ phases parameterized by the mass of the heavy particle insertion and the ratio between the SYK and Maldacena-Qi coupling constants. We then explore Lorentzian time evolution in the baby universe and find a cosine scale factor confirming the big bang / big crunch nature of the bulk cosmology.

### [3] [An exactly solvable obstructed seam on a lattice Klein bottle: bulk-locked Majorana rings and register-dependent toric codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35931)
*Junhao,Wang*

Main category: cond-mat.str-el

TL;DR: 研究在闭合Klein瓶上对Wen plaquette模型作字面反射胶合所产生的阻挫缝合线，并精确求解其边界代数、基态能量、激发gap与简并，同时界定其与拓扑码的关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 考察由反射胶合（reflection gluing）造成的非平凡缝合线（seam）如何在拓扑模型中产生阻挫，并判断该缝合简并是否具有拓扑性质，以便与自洽的toric code实现形成对比。

Method: 分析缝合算符在两个/一个周期上的反对易代数，将其约化为自由Majorana环（free Majorana rings），由对易的体稳定子（bulk stabilizers）锁定允许的环扇区；用相位敏感的Pauli迹（phase-aware Pauli traces）与有限尺寸对角化进行验证；并计算寄存器、逻辑门、距离及手征物质相关量。

Result: 给出精确基态能量、激发gap与简并（含两个24量子比特能量预测）；缝合简并非拓扑性，单格点缝合场可在二阶将其劈裂；边缘寄存器保持任意子标签（anyon labels），Hadamard修饰的格点寄存器表现出颜色依赖的缝合输运，偶周长基态简并为2或4，奇周长存在符号阻挫例外；手征物质选出holonomy双重态，其电场劈裂遵循两条竞争隧穿路径。

Conclusion: 主要成果是含体扇区锁定（bulk sector locking）的精确阻挫缝合解；寄存器、门与物质计算限定了该结果与拓扑码的关系，且物质部分结论仍属有限尺寸陈述。

Abstract: We study a frustrated seam produced by literal reflection gluing of Wen's plaquette model on a closed lattice Klein bottle. The seam operators anticommute along two cycles for even circumference and one cycle of twice the length for odd circumference. Their algebra reduces to free Majorana rings, while the commuting bulk stabilizers constrain the allowed ring sectors. This bulk locking yields exact ground energies, excitation gaps and degeneracies, checked by phase-aware Pauli traces and finite-size diagonalization, including two 24-qubit energy predictions. The seam degeneracy is not topological: the single-site seam fields tested split it at second order. We contrast this obstructed gluing with consistent toric-code realizations. An edge register preserves the anyon labels; a Hadamard-dressed site register has colour-dependent seam transport, with ground-state degeneracy 2 or 4 for even circumference and a sign-frustrated exception at odd circumference. For the enumerated lattice isometries and sizes, we compute logical gates and distances, distinguishing abstract qubit relabelling from local hardware depth. Finally, on three 16- and 24-qubit lattices, chiral matter selects a holonomy doublet whose electric-field splitting follows two competing tunnelling routes. These matter results remain finite-size statements. The main result is the exact obstructed-seam solution with bulk sector locking; the register, gate and matter calculations delimit its relation to topological codes.

### [4] [Accelerating fits in high-energy physics and beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35927)
*Amr Elmarassy,Sebastian Schmitt,Eluned Smith*

Main category: physics.data-an

TL;DR: 该工作总结了粒子物理（particle physics）拟合（fitting）加速技术，比较 Newton、BFGS 与 Migrad 等优化算法的速度与精度，并讨论损失函数（loss function）求值的代数化简、自动微分（automatic differentiation）及 CPU/GPU 现代硬件优化，相关实现集成于 FastFit 库。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 粒子物理数据分析需通过拟合最小化损失函数以估计模型参数；随着数据集增大与分析方法成本上升，参数估计所需时间成为限制因素，因此需要显著加速拟合过程。

Method: 选取具有代表性的一组示例物理拟合模型，比较 Newton 法、BFGS 与 Migrad 等算法变体的速度与精度；并探讨代数化简、自动微分以及 CPU/GPU 混合精度计算（mixed precision）和并行化策略以加速损失函数及其导数求值。

Result: 部分算法带来显著加速：某些 BFGS 变体在各基准上比 Migrad 稳定快约 10 倍且精度相同；对 BFGS、Migrad 等拟牛顿算法（quasi-Newtonian algorithms），Hessian 矩阵初值对运行时间影响很大。损失函数的基本代数化简可显著提升速度与精度；GPU 混合精度在多数加速器上吞吐更高且精度损失不显著。

Conclusion: 算法选择与损失函数求值优化均可有效加速粒子物理拟合，Hessian 初值、代数化简和混合精度是关键因素；全部技术与算法已在 FastFit 库中实现并用于全部基准测试。

Abstract: Data analysis in particle physics often involves optimizing a loss function that quantifies how well a theoretical model describes the observed data, in order to estimate the parameters of the model. This is referred to as fitting. As datasets grow larger and more expensive analysis methods are used, the time required to perform this parameter estimation can be a limiting factor. This work explores techniques that can significantly accelerate the fitting process, and measures their performance on a set of example models chosen to be representative of a variety of physics fits.
  We compare the speed and precision of variants of several well-known optimization algorithms, namely Newton's method, BFGS, and the Migrad algorithm (commonly used in physics fits). We observe significant speedups when using some algorithms: most notably, some variants of BFGS are consistently about $10\times$ faster than Migrad across our benchmarks, while achieving the same precision. For quasi-Newtonian algorithms like BFGS and Migrad, we find that the initial value of the Hessian matrix has a large impact on the runtime.
  We additionally discuss methods for accelerating the evaluation of the loss function and its derivatives, using algebraic simplifications, automatic differentiation, and modern hardware features on the CPU and GPU. We find that basic algebraic simplifications of the loss function can significantly improve speed and precision. On the GPU we explore using mixed precision computation, and show that this achieves much higher throughput on most accelerators without significant precision loss. Lastly we discuss efficient parallelization strategies for both the CPU and the GPU.
  All of the techniques and algorithms in this paper are implemented in our new FastFit library, which was used for all of our benchmarks.

</details>

### [5] [First-order corrections to equatorial periodic orbits around rapidly rotating black holes in dynamical Chern--Simons gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35983)
*Hao-Jie Lin,Tao Zhu,Jing-Fei Zhang,Xin Zhang*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: 该工作计算了动力学 Chern–Simons（dynamical Chern–Simons, dCS）引力中绕快速旋转黑洞赤道周期轨道的一阶能量、径向周期与周向转折半径修正，并分析其符号来源及对波形相位与频谱的影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 需要在强场、快速旋转情形下检验 dCS 引力对黑洞附近周期轨道可观测量的偏离，并厘清周期修正中直接项与能量补偿项的竞争如何决定修正符号，从而为引力检验提供轨道层面的判据。

Method: 采用度规修正的解析拟合，将每条轨道在固定无量纲自旋、无量纲比角动量与有理周期性的条件下匹配到 Kerr 参考轨道；周期修正由固定能量下的直接贡献与保持周期性的能量调整补偿两部分合成。进一步通过双参数扫描定位一阶分数周期修正系数为零的 C_T=0 边界，并用领头四极阶的数值 kludge 波形及其离散谱关联相位累积与谐波频移。

Result: 周期修正的符号随轨道家族与参考路径发生改变：在负周期修正样本中，顺行轨道由能量补偿主导，逆行轨道由直接贡献主导。周向位移与坐标位移可呈相反符号；在固定无量纲轨道参数与小体物理质量下，相位贡献可在一阶精度上通过重标定 Kerr 质量重现。对考察的 C_T=0 轨道，在黑洞质量相等时该相位贡献被抑制，但波形与谱振幅修正仍然非零。

Conclusion: dCS 修正对周期轨道的能量、周期与周向半径具有符号可变的竞争性影响，并通过相位累积与谐波频移体现于波形；周期修正的抑制不意味着波形振幅修正消失，二者需分别对待。

Abstract: We calculate first-order corrections to the energy, radial period, and circumferential turning radii of equatorial periodic orbits around rapidly rotating black holes in dynamical Chern--Simons (dCS) gravity. Using an analytic fit to the metric correction, we match each orbit to a Kerr reference at fixed dimensionless spin, dimensionless specific angular momentum, and rational periodicity. The period correction combines a direct contribution at fixed energy with compensation from the energy adjustment that preserves periodicity. Their competition produces sign changes across orbital families and reference paths. In a two-parameter scan, negative period corrections are dominated by energy compensation for sampled prograde orbits and by the direct contribution for sampled retrograde orbits. We locate $C_T=0$ boundaries, where the coefficient of the first-order fractional period correction vanishes. Circumferential shifts can differ in sign from coordinate shifts. Numerical kludge waveforms at leading quadrupole order and their discrete spectra connect these corrections to phase accumulation and harmonic frequency shifts. At fixed dimensionless orbital parameters and physical mass of the small body, this phase contribution can be reproduced to first order by rescaling the Kerr mass. For the $C_T=0$ orbit examined here, this contribution is suppressed at equal black hole masses, while corrections to the waveform and spectral amplitudes remain nonzero.

### [6] [A gaussian-process reconstruction of gravitational-wave friction from dark standard sirens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36087)
*Michele Mancarella,Viola De Renzis,Alessandro Agapito*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: 该文提出利用暗标准汽笛（dark standard sirens）对引力波（GW）传播中的反常摩擦项（anomalous friction）进行非参数化重建，对 GWTC-5.0 数据未发现偏离广义相对论（general relativity）的证据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准汽笛（standard sirens）可直接探测宇宙距离-红移关系，而许多广义相对论扩展理论（extensions of general relativity）会通过反常摩擦项改变引力波传播振幅，使引力波光度距离（GW luminosity distance）不同于电磁对应体，因此需要数据驱动的检验手段。

Method: 用高斯过程（Gaussian process, GP）非参数化重建摩擦函数 α_M(z)，经积分导出引力波与电磁光度距离之比 Ξ(z)，并保留局部摩擦修正与累积距离效应之间的关系，同时强制引力波光度距离单调且可逆；联合推断源族群（source population）、修改传播与背景宇宙学参数，并引入函数空间诊断（function-space diagnostics）比较柔性与参数化重建。

Result: 在 GWTC-5.0 星表探测的红移范围内，α_M=0 与 Ξ=1 与后验（posterior）一致，未见偏离广义相对论的证据；结果对 GP 核函数、平滑度与振幅先验、分辨率方案及似然（likelihood）处理的变化均保持稳定。

Conclusion: 函数空间诊断（包括先验校准、先验到后验收缩、传播-族群简并的常用汇总及主成分分析（principal-component analysis））可明确指出哪些特征由数据约束、哪些由函数空间先验（function-space prior）所强加，为可解释的引力波传播非参数检验提供了框架。

Abstract: Standard sirens provide a direct probe of the cosmic distance-redshift relation and therefore a powerful test of whether gravitational waves (GWs) propagate as predicted by general relativity on cosmological scales. In many extensions of general relativity, GW propagation is modified by an anomalous friction term, which alters the amplitude evolution and leads to a GW luminosity distance that differs from its electromagnetic counterpart. In this work, we develop a non-parametric reconstruction of this anomalous friction from dark standard sirens. We reconstruct its redshift dependence, encoded in the friction function $α_M(z)$, with a Gaussian process (GP) and derive the corresponding GW-to-EM luminosity-distance ratio $Ξ(z)$ by integration, explicitly retaining the relation between the local friction modification and its accumulated effect on the observed distance, while enforcing a monotonic and invertible GW luminosity distance. We apply the method to the GWTC-5.0 catalog, jointly inferring the source population, modified propagation, and background cosmological parameters. We find no evidence for departures from GR, with $α_M=0$ and $Ξ=1$ consistent with the posterior over the redshift range probed by the catalog. The result is stable under variations of the GP kernel, smoothness and amplitude priors, resolution prescription, and likelihood treatment. We further introduce function-space diagnostics for comparing flexible and parametric reconstructions, including prior calibration, prior-to-posterior contraction, common summaries of propagation-population degeneracies, and principal-component analysis. These tools make explicit which features are informed by the data and which are imposed by the function-space prior, providing a framework for interpretable non-parametric tests of gravitational-wave propagation.
