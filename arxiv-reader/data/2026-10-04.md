### [1] [Infrared Consistency and the Uniqueness of String Amplitudes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02124)
*Yu-tin Huang,Shi-Lin Wan,Zhuo-Hui Wang,Shuang-Yong Zhou*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: 本文在低能展开的所有阶上严格证明了极大超对称（maximal supersymmetry）与六点标量宇称（scalar-parity）条件对四点有效场论振幅施加的非线性约束，由此证实了 Yang–Mills 理论中 Veneziano 振幅与超引力中 Virasoro–Shapiro 振幅的指数形式猜想。

Motivation: 既有研究表明极大超对称结合六点标量宇称条件会对四点有效场论振幅产生非线性约束，数值自举（numerical bootstrap）研究结合幺正性（unitarity）给出了 Veneziano 振幅与 Virasoro–Shapiro 振幅及其无穷自旋替代方案的支持证据；本文旨在将这些约束从低能展开的有限阶推广至所有阶，并给出严格推导。

Method: 采用共线极限（collinear limit），使三个三粒子因子化道（引力情形为六个）同时上壳（on shell）；通过标量宇称投影与单个离散 R 对称性（R-symmetry）消除未知的六点剩余项，得到仅由四点因子化决定的三项主方程；求解该方程并构造由正则前向极限（regular forward limit）重建完整振幅的映射。

Result: 主方程的解证实了所猜想的指数形式，并给出由前向极限重建振幅的映射。在显式解析与正色散（positive dispersive）假设下，规范理论指数中固定的反射偶部分决定前向谱测度（forward spectral measure），唯一选出 Veneziano 振幅，有限自旋留数（finite-spin residues）作为推论出现。引力情形下，指数形式无法确定前向测度：支撑于单一质量层的测度会重建出该质量处无穷多交换自旋的振幅；因此最低质量极点处有限自旋支撑是选取 Virasoro–Shapiro 振幅所需的额外假设。

Conclusion: 该工作将六点标量宇称加极大超对称的非线性约束提升至低能展开所有阶，严格证明指数型振幅形式；规范理论可唯一定出 Veneziano 振幅，而引力中 Virasoro–Shapiro 振幅的选取在给定紫外框架内还需额外假定最低质量极点的有限自旋支撑。

Abstract: Recent work has shown that maximal supersymmetry, combined with a particular scalar-parity condition at six points, imposes nonlinear constraints on four-point effective field theory amplitudes. Numerical bootstrap studies incorporating unitarity provide evidence for the Veneziano amplitude in maximally supersymmetric Yang--Mills theory and for the Virasoro--Shapiro amplitude, together with infinite-spin alternatives, in supergravity. In this paper, we establish these constraints to all orders in the low-energy expansion. The derivation uses a collinear limit in which three three-particle factorization channels, or six in gravity, simultaneously go on shell. A scalar-parity projection and a single discrete R-symmetry eliminate the unknown six-point remainder, leaving three-term master equations determined entirely by four-point factorization. Solving these equations proves the conjectured exponential forms and provides a map that reconstructs the full amplitude from its regular forward limit. Under explicit analytic and positive dispersive assumptions, the fixed reflection-even part of the gauge-theory exponential determines the forward spectral measure and uniquely selects the Veneziano amplitude, with finite-spin residues emerging as a consequence. In gravity, the exponential leaves the forward measure undetermined: a measure supported at a single massive level reconstructs an amplitude with infinitely many exchanged spins at that mass. Finite-spin support at the lowest massive pole is therefore an additional assumption required to select the Virasoro--Shapiro amplitude within the stated ultraviolet framework.
