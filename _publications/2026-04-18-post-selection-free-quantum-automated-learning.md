  A post-selection-free quantum automated learning algorithm that implements
  a complete training path coherently and uses fixed-point amplitude
  amplification to obtain the learned model with high probability. The analysis
  provides output-error and learning-loss guarantees and identifies conditions
  under which coherent resources reduce the cost of restarting failed trajectories.
